FastGen is a PyTorch library for training and distilling diffusion models into fast generative models. Use it to fine-tune a teacher, train a student that generates in fewer steps, and run inference and evaluation.

## Start here

- [Get started](/fastgen/get-started): install FastGen and run the CIFAR-10 training and inference example.
- [Run inference](/fastgen/inference): generate outputs from a teacher or student model.
- [Prepare your dataset](/fastgen/datasets): choose a loader and supply your training data.
- [Troubleshoot a run](/fastgen/troubleshooting): resolve common configuration, memory, and checkpoint issues.

## What you can do

- Distill diffusion models across modalities such as image, video, and audio into students that use fewer generation steps.
- Fine-tune a supported model on your data, including conditional and causal generation tasks.
- Run inference and evaluate teacher and student models.

## Core concepts

A [configuration](/fastgen/configuration) combines a [method](/fastgen/methods), a [network](/fastgen/networks), a [dataset](/fastgen/datasets), and training settings. Experiment configs are runnable starting points; model and data combinations have different prerequisites. Check the selected recipe before starting a large run.
